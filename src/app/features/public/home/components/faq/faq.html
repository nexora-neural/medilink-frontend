What is MediLink?+

MediLink brings health records and medication routines together for patients and healthcare professionals.

Who can create an account?+

Patients, doctors, pharmacists and lab staff can select their role. Professional accounts require verification.

How do I share records with my doctor?+

You review the doctor, information and access period, then confirm before access is granted.

Can I add my own prescription or report?+

Patients can add records. Patient-added prescriptions remain clearly labelled with their original source.
