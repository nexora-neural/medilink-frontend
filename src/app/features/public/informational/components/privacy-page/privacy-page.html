Privacy information

MediLink is a project demonstration. This draft describes the intended handling of information and will be reviewed before a live service is launched.

Information in MediLink

The project is designed to hold account and contact details, patient profile information, and health records such as medications, prescriptions, reports, measurements and vaccinations. Professional profiles and verification details may also be recorded.

Use and sharing

Information is intended to support account access and healthcare workflows. Access to patient records is designed to depend on the user’s role and relevant permissions, including patient consent where required. Patients can review and manage grants to doctors. The project design also records relevant actions in an audit history.

Retention and requests

The current project design retains data and audit history rather than physically deleting them. The process for handling access, correction and deletion requests will be confirmed before launch.

Contact

For privacy questions, email nexora.io.dev@gmail.com.
